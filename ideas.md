# Matrix Website Intelligence Auditor — Design Direction

## Approach 1
**Theme Name:** Graphite Signal

**Very Brief Intro:** A dark, editorial intelligence console with charcoal surfaces, signal-green accents, and an evidence-first rhythm. It feels like a calm control room for making complex website quality legible.

**Probability:** 0.04

## Approach 2
**Theme Name:** Paper Protocol

**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, high-contrast research interface inspired by annotated field notes, technical papers, and modern Swiss information design. It makes the audit feel rigorous, transparent, and human-readable.

**Theme Name:** Mineral Grid

**Very Brief Intro:** A restrained dark UI built around mineral blue, amber, and precise graph-like linework. It communicates systems thinking without leaning on sci-fi glow or decorative dashboard noise.

## Chosen Direction: Paper Protocol

### Design Movement
Contemporary Swiss editorial design fused with research-lab documentation and a subtle brutalist utility layer.

### Core Principles
1. Evidence before ornament: every score is paired with a proof point, status, or inspectable trail.
2. Calm density: the interface should hold complex audit data without feeling crowded.
3. Material contrast: warm paper surfaces, ink-black typography, and red signal marks create hierarchy.
4. Visible structure: rules, labels, section numbers, and graph fragments make the audit method feel tangible.

### Color Philosophy
The base is warm ivory rather than stark white to evoke a printed technical report. Ink black carries primary meaning. Signal red is reserved for defects, active analysis, and high-attention moments; oxidized blue is used for evidence and architecture. A muted sage indicates passing states without turning the system into a generic green dashboard.

### Layout Paradigm
Asymmetric split-screen composition: a persistent left rail anchors the method, while the right workspace shifts between an editorial landing state and a dense results dossier. Major panels align to a vertical measurement line instead of a centered marketing grid.

### Signature Elements
1. Hairline rules with numbered section markers.
2. Small graph/relationship glyphs showing the website intelligence model.
3. “Signal” status chips that combine a colored mark with a short evidence label.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like inspecting a document: expanding a finding reveals its evidence trail, selecting a dimension highlights related nodes, and running an audit turns the method rail into a live progress index. No interaction should feel ornamental or mysterious.

### Animation
Use short, deliberate transitions under 240ms. On analysis, the left rail advances with a crisp red indicator and the score card resolves with a slight upward reveal. Result panels enter with 40ms staggered fades. Hover states use color and underline movement rather than large transforms. Respect reduced motion and keep keyboard flows instant.

### Typography System
Display: Space Grotesk, 700 for large titles and score numerals. Body: IBM Plex Sans, 400–600 for readable analysis text. Labels: IBM Plex Mono, 600 uppercase with generous tracking. Hierarchy is editorial: compact uppercase metadata, wide display headlines, and short readable paragraphs.

### Brand Essence
**Positioning:** A website intelligence auditor for teams who need proof that their digital presence is technically sound, conceptually coherent, and ready for scrutiny.

**Personality:** exacting, lucid, quietly bold.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and diagnostic. CTAs use verbs and describe the action. Microcopy explains what the system can prove and what it cannot infer.

Example lines:
- “Measure the signal behind the surface.”
- “Find the gap between what you claim and what your site supports.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A custom “M” signal mark: three vertical stems connected by a single offset diagonal, suggesting an intelligence graph resolving into a stable matrix. The wordmark uses a tight uppercase grotesk with a red square stop mark; the mark is used independently in the rail and favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Red — #E24B3B**, used sparingly for the active audit state, defects, and the score needle.

## Style Decisions
- Use warm ivory as the primary workspace background, not pure white.
- Keep red exclusive to active state, defect severity, and score emphasis.
- Use editorial labels and measurement rules across landing and results states.
- Prefer asymmetric layouts and evidence-led density over centered hero cards.
